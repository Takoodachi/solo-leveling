How Solo Leveling uses your health data

What it reads

Only your daily step count, through Health Connect. Nothing else: no heart rate, sleep, weight, workouts or food.

Where it goes

Each day's total is saved in the app on your phone and, if you're signed in, in your own private account so it syncs to your other devices. Only you can read it.

What it's used for

Your steps tile, step challenges, the steps chart, and the calories you burn walking. Steps are never shared with friends or on the leaderboard, and nothing is sold or sent anywhere else.

Turning it off

Remove Solo Leveling's access in Health Connect at any time. Steps already imported stay in the app until you delete them.
